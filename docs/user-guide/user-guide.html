Sentinel camera recorder

User guide  ·  for Sentinel {{VERSION}}

The three things you do

  1. Check that it is recording. The Sentinel window says Recording normally or Watching for motion. See part 1.
  2. Change the archive drive on the day you were given, or when the screen says a drive is full. See part 3.
  3. Watch recordings from an archive drive: open the folder 1 - Watch the recordings on it and double-click a day. See part 4.

If the screen shows a problem: do not switch anything off and do not unplug anything. Read the message, write down its code (for example R-003) and the time, and call the person who looks after the system. See part 5. If something went wrong or was done differently, write it down on the Notes page.

1. Is it recording?

Double-click Sentinel on the computer. The window opens on Status. The large words at the top tell you what the recorder is doing:

You can close the Sentinel window. Recording continues with the window closed and while the computer is locked.

2. Leaving the computer

  • Press Lock computer on the Status page (or Windows key + L). Recording continues.
  • Do not close the lid. Do not shut the computer down, put it to sleep or unplug its power.
  • Do not unplug the camera's cable or an archive drive (part 3 says how a drive is taken out).

3. Changing the archive drive

Sentinel copies every finished recording to the archive drive, about once an hour, and checks each copy. Each archive drive has its name on a sticker, for example SSD_1 and SSD_2. The recordings stay on the computer as well until a drive has been taken away.

Taking a drive out and putting the next one in

  1. Open Drives. Under Archive drive every drive that is plugged in has a line with its name and one of: In use, Standing by, Full: press Swap and safely remove, Ready to take out.
  2. Press Swap and safely remove.
  3. Wait. Sentinel first finishes copying to the drive and checks it. This can take several minutes. Do not unplug anything yet.
  4. The page then names the drive and says it is safe to remove. If it asks you to press Safely remove first, press it and wait for the page to say the drive is safe to remove.
  5. Unplug that drive only: the one whose name the page shows. Check the sticker.
  6. Plug in the next drive. Within a minute the page shows it as In use or Standing by.
  7. Fill in the sheet for the drive you took out and hand the drive over as you were told.

If two archive drives are plugged in and one fills up, copying moves to the other one by itself. Nothing has to be pressed for that, and recording is not interrupted.

Never

  • Never unplug a drive that the page has not named as safe to remove.
  • Never use Windows' own "Safely Remove Hardware" instead of the button in Sentinel, unless the page tells you to.
  • Never plug in any drive other than the archive drives with a sticker. A new drive has to be set up first by the person who looks after the system (Erase and set up…).
  • Never delete, rename or move anything on an archive drive.
  • Do not press Cancel swap unless you are told to. It undoes the swap.

If something went differently

  • You unplugged a drive too early, or the wrong one. Plug it in again. Nothing is lost: the recordings are still on the computer, and copying carries on by itself. If the page names two drives, the one to take out is the one it says is ready. Then write a note (part 5).
  • A drive came back from the office. Plug it in. Its line reads Back from the office: return it to service before it is used again. That step (Return to service…) is for the person who looks after the system, after the office has copied the drive and checked the copy.
  • The screen shows code M-007 (the drive could not be safely removed). Do not unplug the drive. Read the line under the code: it says why, and what to do. To take out an archive drive that Sentinel is using, the button is always Swap and safely remove.
  • No drive is plugged in for a while. Recording continues; the recordings wait on the computer and are copied when a drive is put in.

4. Watching recordings

From an archive drive, or from the copy of one that the office made:

  1. Open the drive (or the folder the office copied it into).
  2. Open the folder 1 - Watch the recordings.
  3. Double-click a day, for example 2026-11-18 Wednesday - CAM01. The whole day plays in order, one hour after the other. If Windows asks which program to use, choose VLC media player.
  4. In the player's list every line is one recording, with the time it starts and ends. To go to a time of day, pick the line for that hour.
  • Leave the folder Sentinel on the drive alone. It holds the original recordings and the lists that prove they are unchanged. Nobody needs to open it to watch.
  • The file START HERE.txt on the drive says the same in a few lines.
  • A day that is not in the folder has no recordings on that drive.
  • The newest hour appears once its recording is finished and has been copied and checked.
  • On a drive last written by a version before 0.3.22 the day files are in the folder Sentinel, then the camera's folder, then Playlists.

The Recordings page of the Sentinel window shows when there are recordings, where there are gaps and why, and when there was motion. It does not play video.

To give recordings to a reviewer, a review drive is made on the Export page (Export new recordings). Do this only if it is part of your instructions.

5. When the screen shows a problem

A problem is shown in plain words with a code, for example:

Recording stopped. Error R-003: Camera connection lost. Automatic recovery unsuccessful. Contact technical support and provide diagnostic report.

  1. Do not switch the computer off, do not restart it and do not unplug anything. Sentinel keeps trying by itself, and many problems go away again.
  2. Write down the code and the time.
  3. Press Diagnostic report… on the Status page and save the report where it suggests. Send it if you are asked to.
  4. Call the person who looks after the system and read them the code. The list at the end of this guide says what each code means.

The word beside a message says how urgent it is: Notice and Recovering automatically need nothing from you; Attention needed means recording continues but someone should look; Action required and Recording stopped mean call now.

Writing a note

If something went wrong or was done differently, write it down, so that there is a reason on record:

  1. Open Notes.
  2. Fill in Who (your name), What happened, When, Where and Why.
  3. Press Save note. The note appears under Saved notes with the time it was saved. It cannot be changed or deleted; to correct it, save another note.

After testing, before the real recordings start

Never delete recordings by hand, not from the computer and not from an archive drive. Sentinel keeps a log of every recording and reports each missing one (codes V-001, A-006, A-008), with no way to clear it. To remove test recordings there is one step, done once:

  1. Close the Sentinel window. Plug in every archive drive.
  2. Right-click START-A-NEW-RECORDING-LOG.cmd (in the folder the program came in), choose "Run as administrator".
  3. Press a key once when it asks, then wait. For up to a minute little happens: Sentinel is counting what is on the computer and on the drives. Keys pressed while you wait are ignored.
  4. It shows what will be set aside and removed, and asks. Type the words it names (NEW LOG), then your name. Nothing is removed before that.
  5. Read the last lines: DONE, or NOT DONE and why. The window stays open until you type CLOSE.
  6. Open Sentinel again. Opening it starts recording.

The step sets the recording log aside (it is kept, never deleted), deletes the recordings on the computer, empties the archive drives that are plugged in (they keep their names) and starts a new, empty log whose first entry records the step. Settings are not changed. Every run, done or not, is written to the file new-recording-log.log in Sentinel's log folder and is in the diagnostic report. Without the file, the same is done by the command "Sentinel.exe new-recording-log" in a Command Prompt opened as administrator.

Once only. If the step was done on this computer before, what has been recorded since is most likely real. Sentinel then says so in lines marked !!, and NEW LOG is no longer enough: it asks for words that name the number of recordings that would be deleted. If any of them may be needed, type NO.

The file READ-ME-FIRST.txt beside the program lists the commands for technical support.

7. Messages and their codes

The first letter of a code says what it is about. Codes do not change their meaning between versions.

8. What changed

Changes that matter to the people who use the recorder, newest first.

0.3.29

  • For the person who looks after the system: START-A-NEW-RECORDING-LOG.cmd can no longer end without being read. A key pressed before its question is on the screen is ignored; Enter alone is asked again instead of cancelling; its window stays open until CLOSE is typed; and every run is written to new-recording-log.log in Sentinel's log folder. See part 6.
  • The same step cannot be repeated by habit: on a computer where it was done before, it warns that the recordings are most likely real and asks for different words. See part 6.
  • For technical support: a recording that lost pictures because the camera's data arrived damaged is now marked in the recording log, also when the loss is too short to count as a break. Nothing changes on the screen; the marks are in the diagnostic report.

0.3.28

  • Recordings: a recording no longer shows a start time from before the recorder was started, or a gap of hours inside it that never happened. The camera changes its own time labels a few seconds after it is connected to; Sentinel now keeps the true times. No picture was ever missing.
  • Drives: if the wrong drive is unplugged after Swap and safely remove, the page now says which drive was unplugged, which one should have been, and to plug the first one back in. It used to show nothing.

0.3.27

  • New page Notes: write down who, what, when, where and why when something went wrong or was done differently, and press Save note. See part 5.
  • For the person who looks after the system: test recordings are cleared with one step, START-A-NEW-RECORDING-LOG.cmd, instead of deleting them by hand. See part 6.
  • Drives: the list Drives plugged in no longer offers Safely remove… for an archive drive that Sentinel is using. That button could not take such a drive out and answered with code M-007. The button for it is Swap and safely remove, as before, and the line under the list now says so.
  • Code M-007 no longer says to close open files when that is not the reason. The line under the code says why the drive was not removed and what to do.
  • For the person who looks after the system: a drive that was taken out with Swap and safely remove and plugged in again can be taken out again with Safely remove…. Every removal that did not happen is now in the recording log and in the diagnostic report, with its reason.

0.3.26

  • The computer makes room earlier: recordings already on an archive drive that has been taken out are deleted when less than 150 GB is free, not only when the storage warning is reached (Settings, Storage). Which recordings are deleted has not changed.
  • Status: the days of recording left count what can still be deleted, and Details has a new line Kept on this computer.

0.3.25

  • After the recorder has been stopped on purpose (Stop recorder… on the System page), the Status page keeps saying Not recording and says when it was stopped. It used to change to System error after a few seconds and show the camera as disconnected, although nothing was wrong.
  • New button Start recording on the Status page, under the words Not recording. It starts the recorder again. It is only there while the recorder is stopped.

0.3.24

  • When a new version is put on the recorder from a folder, the screens and the message afterwards now say that the version is being installed. They used to say that Sentinel was "going back", which sounded as if something had gone wrong. Nothing had: only the words have changed.

0.3.23

  • This guide. It is the file "Sentinel User Guide.pdf" in the program folder, and User guide (PDF) in the Help menu opens it.

0.3.22

  • Archive drives: the day files to watch are now in the folder 1 - Watch the recordings at the top of the drive, named by date and weekday, with START HERE.txt beside it. A drive that is already in use changes over by itself.
  • Settings, Camera: Test picture and sound.
  • Settings, Motion: the Lighting option. The room light being switched on or off counts as motion at once.

0.3.21

  • Settings, Motion: If no motion is seen for a long time. When it switches on, the screen shows code W-003 and the recorder records all the time until it sees motion again.

0.3.20

  • Settings, Camera: Record the camera's sound with the video. Off unless switched on. Codes N-001 and N-002 are about sound.

0.3.19

  • Settings, Motion: the live test shows its lines while it runs.

0.3.17

  • Drives: with two archive drives plugged in, the page says that copying moves to the second drive by itself when the first is full.
